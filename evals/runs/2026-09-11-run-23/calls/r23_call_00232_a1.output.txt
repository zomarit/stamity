## Ranked hypotheses and the observation each still needs

**1. Connection-pool exhaustion under concurrent checkouts.**
Needs: pool active-count and acquire-wait time sampled at the timed-out request, plus concurrent checkout count over the preceding 60s.
Confirms if: timeouts land while the pool is at max with nonzero acquire wait, and cluster with concurrency peaks.
Disconfirms if: the pool has headroom at the moment of timeout.

**2. Slow third-party payment call under a specific card type.**
Needs: per-call latency of the outbound payment call on timed-out requests, tagged by card brand/BIN, against the baseline latency distribution.
Confirms if: timed-out requests cluster on one card type with call latency at or over the request budget.
Disconfirms if: card type is spread as normal and payment latency is unremarkable on the failures.

One capture window separates these two — they predict different things at the same instant.
