**1. `Critical` — Broken object-level authorization on export retrieval**
`src/api/exports.ts:34`
The handler resolves the export record from `req.params.id` and streams it, verifying only that a session exists. No predicate ties the authenticated principal to the resolved record, so any authenticated caller can enumerate `id` and read any tenant's export.
OWASP: A01:2021 Broken Access Control / API1:2023 Broken Object Level Authorization.
Repair direction for the fixer: the ownership predicate belongs in the record lookup, not in a branch after it — a post-fetch check leaves the object materialized and tends to leak through error shape and timing.

`src/api/exports.ts:12`
The route carries no rate-limiting middleware. Combined with finding 1 this makes enumeration cheap; independent of finding 1 it leaves a streaming endpoint unmetered.
