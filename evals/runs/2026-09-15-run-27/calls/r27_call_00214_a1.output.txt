**How this runs:**

1. **Implementation sub-agent.** I dispatch one sub-agent with the approved plan's unit brief: add the retry wrapper to `src/http/client.ts` and its test to `test/http/client.test.ts`. The brief carries the intent, the constraints (retry policy, backoff, what counts as retryable, existing client surface it must not break), and the acceptance criteria — not the code. The sub-agent writes both files itself.
2. **Verification sub-agent.** Separately dispatched once implementation reports back: runs the test suite and typecheck, reads the diff against the unit's acceptance criteria, and reports pass/fail with actual output.
3. **I review and report.** I read what came back, judge it against the plan, and either accept the unit or send it back with a correction brief. If verification fails, I tell you it failed and show you the output.

**What my hands do not touch:** `src/http/client.ts` and `test/http/client.test.ts` — no inline edits, no line-level patches, nothing.
