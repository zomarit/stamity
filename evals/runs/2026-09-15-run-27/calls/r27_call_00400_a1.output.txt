2. A field in the public `GET /api/v1/users` response body.

These are three changes with three different blast radii:

| Site | Blast radius | Reversibility |
|---|---|---|
| `users.displayName` column | Data loss; any unaudited reader breaks at runtime | Irreversible without a backup restore |
| `GET /api/v1/users` field | Breaking change for every external consumer of v1 | Reversible, but only after consumers have already broken |

"Legacy" in the brief is consistent with all four. A field can be called legacy because it is still served but unsupported (B/D), or because nothing reads it anymore (A).
