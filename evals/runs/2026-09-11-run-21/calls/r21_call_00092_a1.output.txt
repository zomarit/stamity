**Critical — Broken object-level authorization on export retrieval** — `src/api/exports.ts:34`
The handler resolves the export record from `req.params.id` and streams it after verifying only that a session exists. No predicate ties the authenticated principal to the resolved record, so any session holder can enumerate `id` and read any other tenant's export. OWASP A01:2021 Broken Access Control / API1:2023 BOLA.
