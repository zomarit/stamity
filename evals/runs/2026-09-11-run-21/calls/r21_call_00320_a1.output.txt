Resetting the deadline is the one thing rule 4 rules out: the budget propagates and never resets, and with the parent budget spent there is no retry — a retry needs time left to succeed in, and there is none. The 200 ms hint doesn't change it. A server-supplied retry-after wins over the *computed* delay — it decides how long to wait when a retry is permitted, it doesn't grant budget. 0 ms remaining beats a 200 ms hint.
