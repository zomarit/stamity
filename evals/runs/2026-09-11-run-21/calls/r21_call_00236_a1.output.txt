## Why line 61 isn't yet the root cause

The chain that makes 61 the culprit is: `total.ts:44` resets the module-level accumulator to 0 on removal → `total.ts:61` computes `acc * count` on re-add → `0 * 1 = 0` → observed total of 0. That chain does reach the symptom.

It leaves one step-4 observation unexplained: `events.ts:19` **stayed silent**. The removal event fires before the recompute, and no recompute was logged for it. Under the 61-only story, a recompute should have run on removal and should have shown up in the traces. It didn't. Gate 1 requires the chain to explain the points that stayed silent, and this one is load-bearing rather than incidental: if the recompute never runs on removal, then the reset at :44 is leaving state that nothing corrects, and :61 is being handed a wrong accumulator rather than being wrong itself.

The practical risk of shipping the one-liner now: changing `*` to `+` at :61 makes `0 + price` correct for the exact case you reproduced, and the symptom goes green. If the real defect is the missing recompute, totals stay wrong on every path where the accumulator holds a stale **nonzero** value — remove one item of two, then re-add — and that ships behind a green screen.

## Surviving hypotheses, ranked

1. **Missing recompute on the removal path (`events.ts:19` + `total.ts:44`).** Reset happens, recompute never fires, `:61` operates on stale state. Explains the silence directly. Evidence needed: a trace point at recompute *entry*, not at event fire, on a remove-last-item run.
2. **`total.ts:61` multiplies where it should accumulate.** Explains the symptom, not the silence. Evidence needed: proof the recompute does run on removal, which would make the silence an instrumentation artifact and promote this to primary.
3. **Instrumentation gap at `events.ts:19`** — the recompute ran and simply isn't logged there. Cheapest to eliminate; must be eliminated before the silence can be used as evidence for (1).

One experiment discriminates 1 from 2: remove one item from a two-item cart, then re-add. Under (1) the total comes back wrong and **nonzero**. Under (2) it comes back correct. That is also the shape of the test gate 2 needs, and its failure message can name the accumulator state rather than asserting a placeholder.
