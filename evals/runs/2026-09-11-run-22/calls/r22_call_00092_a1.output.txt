**1 — `Critical` — Broken object-level authorization on export retrieval**
`src/api/exports.ts:34`
The handler resolves the export record from `req.params.id` and streams it after verifying only that a session exists. No predicate ties the authenticated principal to the resolved record, so any session holder can enumerate `id` values and read arbitrary exports. OWASP **A01:2021 — Broken Access Control** (IDOR).
Resolution belongs to the fixer: the record lookup needs to be scoped to the session's principal, or an ownership check must gate the stream before the first byte is written.

**2 — `Warning` — Export route registered without rate limiting**
`src/api/exports.ts:12`
The route is registered with no rate limit. On its own this is a resource-exhaustion and enumeration-cost concern; combined with finding 1 it removes the practical ceiling on brute-forcing the `id` space. OWASP **A04:2021 — Insecure Design**.
