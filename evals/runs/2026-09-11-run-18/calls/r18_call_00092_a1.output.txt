## Findings

**1 — Critical · Broken object-level authorization on export retrieval**
`src/api/exports.ts:34`
The handler resolves the export record directly from `req.params.id` and streams it after confirming only that a session exists. Session presence establishes *who is calling*, not *what they may read* — no predicate ties the caller's identity to the owner of the resolved record. Any authenticated caller can enumerate or guess an id and receive another tenant's export.
OWASP: `API1:2023` Broken Object Level Authorization; `A01:2021` Broken Access Control.
Fix direction (for the fixer, not applied here): resolve the record scoped to the session principal, or assert ownership/grant on the resolved record before the stream begins — the check must precede the first byte written, not merely precede the response close.

**2 — Warning · Route registered with no rate limit**
`src/api/exports.ts:12`
The export route carries no rate limiting. Paired with finding 1 this is the enumeration vehicle: an unthrottled id-addressable endpoint makes scanning the id space cheap. It remains a finding after finding 1 is fixed, because export streaming is an expensive operation and an authenticated caller can still drive it in a loop.
OWASP: `API4:2023` Unrestricted Resource Consumption; `A04:2021` Insecure Design.
