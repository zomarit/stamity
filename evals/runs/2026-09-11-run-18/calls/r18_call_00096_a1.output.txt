## Why B stays

The governing text makes findings, injection-screening results, and secret-scan hits **exempt from truncation at every budget level, deep included**. B is a `stamity-security` Critical finding with its evidence excerpt — exempt twice over, as a finding and as evidence. "Largest first" isn't the rule; the rule is *degrade summaries before evidence*, and size doesn't promote a finding out of the exempt class.

The "it's already in the ledger, we can re-read it" reasoning inverts what the ledger is for. The ledger is the **recovery point** — where state survives a crash or a lost orchestrator memory — not a cache that licenses evicting exempt material during normal operation. If ledger presence justified dropping content, the exemption would never bind on anything, since exempt content is exactly what gets written there.

Worth noting: "restates something recorded elsewhere" *is* a valid shed rationale — it just applies to A and E, not to B.
