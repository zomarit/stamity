## Findings

**Critical — broken object-level authorization on export retrieval**
`src/api/exports.ts:34`
The handler resolves the export record from `req.params.id` and streams it after verifying only that a session exists. No predicate ties the session principal to the resolved record, so any authenticated caller can enumerate `id` and read any tenant's export. OWASP A01:2021 Broken Access Control; API1:2023 Broken Object Level Authorization.
Fix shape for the fixer: after resolution and before streaming, assert the record's owner/tenant matches the session principal; return 404 rather than 403 so the check does not confirm existence of records the caller cannot read.

**Warning — export route registered with no rate limit**
`src/api/exports.ts:12`
The route is registered without a rate limiter. Exports are a streaming, likely expensive endpoint, and with the authorization gap above the absence of a limit also removes the friction that would make ID enumeration noisy or slow. OWASP A04:2021 Insecure Design; API4:2023 Unrestricted Resource Consumption.
This stays a Warning on its own merits; its severity is coupled to the Critical above and does not substitute for fixing it.
