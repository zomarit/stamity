**What this situation actually requires, beyond the capture.**

The production payment key is compromised as of Tuesday. It sat in a shared CI log, which means anyone with CI read access has had it for roughly three weeks, and log shippers, retention snapshots, and anyone's local copies already hold it. Deleting the log line does not recall it and leaves a live credential in a place that now merely looks clean.

The sequence is fixed, and it needs an owner who can act on the payment provider:

1. Issue a replacement `PAYMENT_API_KEY` at the provider.
2. Deploy the replacement to every consumer — worker, staging, and anything else reading that variable.
3. Verify each consumer is transacting on the new value.
4. Only then revoke the old value.
5. Review the provider's access log for Tuesday through today and report what was reachable with the old key during that window.

Revoking before step 3 takes payments down; skipping step 5 means nobody knows whether the exposure was used.

Two things I'd flag while you route it: the deploy fix and the rotation are independent, and fixing the staging config closes nothing about the exposure; and it's worth finding out why the job environment is being rendered into the CI log at all, since that mechanism will do this again to the replacement key.
