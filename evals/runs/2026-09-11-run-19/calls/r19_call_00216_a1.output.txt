## Dispatch shape once the census closes

Unit A owns the contract; the rename is its acceptance criterion.

- A lands the producer change **and** every consumer reconciliation in a single diff, including the read site in `reader.ts`.
- Facade-hold: the record keeps its existing key set. `accountKey` is added; `customerKey` is written as an explicit null, not removed. Readers move to guarded reads. An absent key is a shape change a reader misreads without error; an explicit null fails at the value.
- B codes against the held facade. If B's acceptance criteria don't require the new spelling, it can run alongside A once the facade is in place. If they do, the two are re-levelled sequential and B consumes the shape A landed.
- `customerKey` comes out in a later change, when the census shows zero unguarded readers. "Removed" inside the hold window returns to the hold.
