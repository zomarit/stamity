The failure mode if both run is the silent-wrong one, which is the Critical case: after A lands, the stored record has no `customerKey`, and `src/reader.ts` reads absent rather than erroring. No build break, no exception — just a wrong value downstream.

Two ways forward.

**Serialise** — the stated fallback. A lands the rename plus every consumer reconciliation in one diff; B is re-levelled after it and codes against `accountKey` as landed. Simplest, and correct without needing the facade machinery.

**Census then facade-hold** — if the two need to overlap in time. Run the repo-wide search over both spellings first, then A takes ownership (its acceptance criteria require the rename) and lands under a hold: the persisted shape keeps `customerKey` alongside the new `accountKey`, with `customerKey` written as an explicit null once B's reads are guarded rather than dropped outright. B moves to guarded reads against the held shape. `customerKey` comes out in a later change, once the census shows zero unguarded readers.

I'd recommend serialising. The parallelism buys one unit's worth of wall-clock here, and the hold plus its later removal change is more moving parts than that's worth for a two-unit batch.
