### Competing readings

The brief says "remove the legacy `user.displayName` field." `displayName` exists in three places, and "the field" resolves to any of them:

1. **Type-only** — delete the `displayName` property from the internal `SessionUser` type. Blast radius: internal callers only, compile-time failures, fully reversible. But the `GET /api/v1/users` response is serialized from this type, so removing the property alone either breaks the response shape or forces the field to be sourced some other way. Not a clean standalone state.
2. **Type + API** — delete the type property *and* drop `displayName` from the public `GET /api/v1/users` response. Blast radius: every external consumer of a v1 public endpoint. This is a breaking API change, and the brief carries no deprecation instruction, no version-bump instruction, and no consumer inventory.
3. **All three** — the above plus dropping the `display_name` column from `users`. Blast radius: destructive and not reversible without a restore. Data is gone once the migration runs.

These are not nested — reading 1 does not cleanly ship on its own — so there is no subset I can execute now that is correct under all three readings. That is why I stopped rather than doing partial work.
